# VHOX Master Brief v1

## Source of truth

This document records the brand and product decisions explicitly approved for the repository. When a commercial fact is absent here, the website must label it as pending instead of inferring or fabricating it.

## Brand

- Brand: **VHOX**.
- Positioning: independent premium streetwear house.
- Core idea: **Movimiento Exclusivo**.
- Signature: **From a distance, black. Up close, VHOX.**
- Secondary signature, when appropriate: **Just be yourself.**
- Promise: identity, material, fit, detail and belonging.
- Visual direction: dark, human, editorial, monumental, precise and product-led.
- Palette: deep black, bone white, controlled greys and the official VHOX green as a restrained brand accent.
- Official identity: the existing VHOX logo in `public/brand/vhox-logo-source.png`. Do not redesign it.

## Chromatic Black

VHOX pieces read as black from a distance and reveal a dark undertone through light, angle, proximity or movement. The website may explain this direction visually, but it must not claim a finalized textile technology, performance result or physical property until a real sample validates it.

## Customer understanding

Within five seconds, a first-time visitor should understand:

1. VHOX is a premium streetwear brand.
2. Chromatic Black is the visual difference.
3. DROP 001 is SIGNAL.
4. The collection is the primary route to discover the pieces.
5. Any unconfirmed release or commerce information is stated honestly.

Primary journey:

**Discover → Desire → Understand the product → Resolve questions → Trust → Buy or request confirmed release information.**

## Product truth policy

The following fields must be supplied and approved before a product becomes purchasable or receives `Product` / `Offer` structured data:

- confirmed name and product imagery;
- confirmed price and currency;
- real availability;
- color and undertone;
- composition and weight;
- fit and size guide;
- construction or technique;
- care instructions;
- delivery information;
- changes and returns policy;
- approved purchase destination.

Do not invent quantities, scarcity, dates, prices, materials, waterproofing, technical performance, shipping coverage, fulfillment times, restock policy, reviews, testimonials or certifications.

## Experience principles

- The first image creates desire; later images demonstrate silhouette, color, surface and detail.
- Editorial imagery may use `object-fit: cover` when the crop is intentional.
- Product imagery must preserve the complete garment with `contain` or an approved per-image crop.
- Motion is slow, restrained and informative. It must not hide or compete with the product.
- No terminal, crypto, server, executable-code, particle-simulator or videogame language.
- No fake checkout, fake inventory, fake waitlist confirmation or fake social proof.
- Mobile, tablet, desktop, keyboard navigation and reduced motion are first-class requirements.
